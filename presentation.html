How AarogyaFlow AI Works

3 simple proactive steps connecting 25,000+ PHCs into a smart healthcare grid.

1. Real-Time Tracking
Monitors daily medicine bin-cards, available beds (General & Oxygen), doctor attendance by speciality, and patient footfall velocity.
2. Gemini AI Forecasting
Analyzes seasonal spikes, local viral outbreaks, and consumption trends to predict exact days of stock remaining (7, 15, 30 days).
3. Smart Redistribution
Finds the nearest clinic with surplus stock and creates an instant transfer order (e.g., 500 ORS packets from Ara to Buxar, 38 km away).
Rohit Speaks (55 sec)

Second, our Google Gemini AI engine predicts the exact date of depletion based on seasonal illness spikes. And third, it doesn't just show an alarm — it finds a nearby hospital with extra stock and prepares a transfer plan so medicines arrive before the shelf goes empty."

Data Privacy & Federated Architecture

Patient confidentiality is guaranteed by design (DPDP Act 2023 compliant).

🛡️ Zero-PII Policy
We NEVER collect patient names, Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, or individual medical files. Only aggregated resource counts are processed.
🧠 Federated Edge AI
Raw clinical records stay locked inside local district servers. Only mathematical model weight updates are shared using Differential Privacy.
🇮🇳 data.gov.in Integration
Direct connection to open datasets (Rural Health Statistics & NLEM Essential Medicines) with certified fallback if government servers are down.
Tech Stack: TypeScript · Vite · Leaflet (OpenStreetMap) · Chart.js · Web Speech API · Google Gemini AI (Backend Proxy, Zero Hardcoded Secrets) · Node Express
Rohit Speaks (55 sec)
"The biggest concern in healthcare is privacy. In AarogyaFlow, patient privacy is non-negotiable. We collect zero patient names or Aadhaar numbers. Under Federated Learning, sensitive patient data never leaves the hospital server — only mathematical insights are shared. Furthermore, all AI queries pass through a secure backend proxy so our API keys are never exposed in frontend code."
